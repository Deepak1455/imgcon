Contact ImgCon

We welcome questions about using the image tools, reports about technical problems, accessibility feedback, and privacy requests. ImgCon is maintained as a small web project, and email is the direct support channel.

Email support

imgcon72@gmail.com

We aim to reply within two business days. Response time may be longer during holidays or periods of high message volume.

This contact page does not submit messages to a server. Selecting the email link opens your device's configured email application. If no email application is configured, copy the address and send a message using your preferred email service.

What to include in a support email

To help us understand a problem, include the page you were using, the browser and device type, what you expected to happen, and the steps that caused the issue. If there is an error message, you may include its text. Please do not email passwords, identity documents, private photos or other sensitive information. Image files are processed on your device and are not needed for a support request.

Privacy and legal questions

For a privacy request, describe the right you wish to exercise and any relevant email correspondence. Review the Privacy Policy for information about local image processing, website providers and advertising technologies. For questions about permitted use, see the Terms of Service.
