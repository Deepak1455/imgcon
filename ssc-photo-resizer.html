How to resize an SSC photo or signature

  1. Read the current SSC notification and note the separate photo and signature constraints.
  2. Select the matching preset and image; adjust its size, crop, dimensions or output format to match the notice.
  3. Download the result and verify its actual size, dimensions, format and legibility before uploading.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the SSC portal reject my photo?

The size, pixel dimensions, format, image quality or framing may not match the current application's rules. Check its validation message and active notice.

How can I resize an SSC signature under 10KB?

Use the signature preset, which starts at a 10KB target and 138 × 177 pixels, then confirm that the limit and dimensions match your form. Adjust if necessary and keep the strokes clear.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing occurs locally in your browser and selected images are not sent to ImgCon's image-processing server.

Can I use the same file for every SSC exam?

Not necessarily. Requirements may vary by exam, recruitment cycle and upload field. Check the current instructions each time.
