    Why convert images to WebP?

    WebP can encode photos and graphics more efficiently than some older image formats while supporting lossy and lossless compression. A smaller image can reduce page transfer size and help a website load faster, depending on the source image, quality settings, browser support, and delivery setup. Convert a JPG or PNG to WebP, compare the preview and file size, and choose the format that best fits your site.
