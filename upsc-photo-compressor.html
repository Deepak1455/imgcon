Three steps to prepare a UPSC image

  1. Read the current exam notification and identify separate photo and signature requirements.
  2. Load the image into ImgCon and adjust the preset target, dimensions, crop and format to match those rules.
  3. Download and inspect the file, checking file size, dimensions and legibility before application upload.

Frequently asked questions

Why might the UPSC portal reject my photo or signature?

The file may fail a size, dimension, format or image-quality requirement. Check the current exam notification and the portal's error message.

How do I resize a photo to at least 350 × 350 pixels?

The UPSC preset starts with a 350 × 350 output. Use the crop and resize controls to meet the current notice; verify the exported dimensions after download.

Can I compress my signature under 20KB?

Choose the signature image and set a custom target that meets the current notification. Make sure the final signature remains legible.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. ImgCon processes the selected image on your device in the browser; it is not sent to an ImgCon image-processing server.
