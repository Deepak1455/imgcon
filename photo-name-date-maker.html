How to add a name and date to an exam photo

  1. Read the current recruitment notice to confirm that the name/date caption is required and note its exact format.
  2. Select a clear passport photo, enter your name and date, and choose the matching date prefix. The name is automatically converted to uppercase.
  3. Review the preview, choose a compression target if needed, and download the JPEG. Check its actual size, dimensions, and appearance before uploading.

Frequently asked questions

Why do SSC forms reject a photo without a name and date?

Some exam notices require the candidate name and date of photograph on a clear strip. Requirements can change; consult the active SSC notice and the upload screen.

What date format should I use?

Select the prefix and DD-MM-YYYY date format specified by the application. The picker defaults to today's date; change it to the actual photo date when required.

Does this tool upload my photo?

No. Your photo and text are drawn on a canvas in your browser and are not uploaded to ImgCon's image-processing server.

Can I compress the photo to 20KB or 50KB?

Yes. Choose either target, and the tool adjusts JPEG quality and, if needed, pixel dimensions. Very complex images may not reach the target without substantial quality loss; the actual resulting size is shown.
