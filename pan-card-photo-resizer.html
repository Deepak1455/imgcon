Three steps to prepare your PAN images

  1. Check the current NSDL or UTIITSL rules for the photo and signature fields.
  2. Select the photo or signature preset and adjust the target and output settings to match those instructions.
  3. Download and verify each output separately, including dimensions, format, size and legibility.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my PAN application reject the photo?

Verify the current upload size, format, pixel dimensions, background and photo instructions for the specific service and application. A 213 × 213 image may still fail another requirement.

How do I resize a PAN photo to 213 × 213 pixels?

Use the photo preset, select an image and review the 213 × 213 output. Confirm the file size and other requirements on your current upload form.

How can I reduce my signature to 10–20KB?

Use the signature preset and enter the size limit specified by your application. Check the downloaded signature is both within the limit and legible.

Are my PAN images uploaded to ImgCon?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to ImgCon's image-processing server.
