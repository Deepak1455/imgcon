How to Resize Your Signature under 20KB in 3 Steps

  1. Prepare a clean signature. Sign with black or blue ink on plain white paper using a clear, even light. Let the ink dry, and avoid shadows, folds or marks near the signature.
  2. Crop and resize in ImgCon. Select your scan or photo. The editor starts at a 2:1 crop and JPEG with a 20KB maximum. Adjust the crop to include the full signature without excess margins; use the contrast and sharpness controls to keep the strokes distinct.
  3. Download and check the result. Confirm the file size, dimensions and format against the current exam application. If a form requires at least 10KB, make sure the output is not below that minimum; do not rely on a preset instead of the official requirements.

Tips for a clear, accepted signature

  • Use plain white paper and a dark black or blue pen.
  • Keep the paper flat and use even lighting to avoid shadows.
  • Crop closely but leave enough margin to preserve every signature stroke.
  • Check the file size, pixel dimensions, format and legibility after downloading.
  • Use a signature that matches your official records and follow the portal's current instructions.
